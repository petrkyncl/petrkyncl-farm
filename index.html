02Recents

A memory for your phone.

A 3 mm MagSafe card records your calls. The app keeps your notifications and your email too, and AI turns all of it into one memory you can ask. “What did the dentist tell me?” It knows.

03Wear Close

A pair of bracelets. One tap says I miss you.

A pair of bracelets with an NFC chip sealed inside and nothing to charge. Bring your phone to yours and theirs lights up: “Petr is thinking about you.” The same tap can carry a photo or a mood into your shared memories. iOS and Android.

04Internát

A dormitory on autopilot.

Students stopped signing a paper book to go out. They ask for a later return in the app, a parent approves it with one tap, and the chip at the door tells the night shift who's back.

05So far

From Fiverr orders to a phone farm.

    06Smíchovská

    No free places. I kept showing up anyway.

    After a year at a school in Jičín, I wanted somewhere harder. Smíchovská, the best IT school in the country, had no free places. I kept showing up anyway: in the director's office with TaskerProjects, at their events. At the end of the summer we were sitting across from each other when he found out a place had just opened. He said he'd take me. I asked if it was really that simple. He nodded at the pile of motivation letters on his desk: “Or should I go through those 400?”

    07Hackathon

    Cursor Hackathon, Prague, September 2026. Eighty people, eighty projects, five hours to build yours and then pitch it on stage, in English. I was seventeen.
