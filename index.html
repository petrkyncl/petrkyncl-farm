02Recents

A memory for your phone.

A 3 mm MagSafe card records your calls. The app keeps your notifications and your email too, and AI turns all of it into one memory you can ask. “What did the dentist tell me?” It knows.

03Wear Close

A pair of bracelets. One tap says I miss you.

A pair of bracelets with an NFC chip sealed inside and nothing to charge. Bring your phone to yours and theirs lights up: “Petr is thinking about you.”

A photo or a note can go with it, and every moment is kept in your shared Memories. iOS and Android.

    06Smíchovská střední

    No free places. I kept showing up anyway.

    My first year was at a school in Jičín where nobody expected much from us. I wanted out.

    Smíchovská střední was a different world: the right people, the right direction. It had no free places.

    So I kept coming back: to the director's office with TaskerProjects, to their events.

    At the end of the summer we were sitting across from each other when he found out a place had just opened. He looked at me like I'd just got lucky.

    I asked if that meant it was official, if I could count on it. He nodded at the pile of motivation letters on his desk: “Should I go through those 400?”

    07Hackathon

    I showed up with a fever and left with second place.

    Cursor Hackathon, Prague, September 2026. Eighty people, eighty projects, five hours to build yours and then pitch it on stage, in English. I was seventeen.
